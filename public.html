Comprehensive Eye Check-ups

  • Home
  • Public

Our hospital serves the general public with top-tier eye care services. We aim to make advanced eye care accessible and affordable to everyone. Below are the detailed services we offer to the public:

  • Routine vision screenings for all age groups.
  • Detailed evaluation for refractive errors such as myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism.
  • Pediatric eye check-ups for early detection of vision problems.
  • Geriatric eye exams focusing on age-related conditions like cataracts and macular degeneration.
  • Diabetic eye check-ups to monitor and manage diabetic retinopathy.
  • Glaucoma screenings to detect and manage intraocular pressure.
  • Specialized screenings for dry eyes, red eyes, and other common conditions.
    Advanced Diagnostics
  • State-of-the-art imaging techniques, including OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography) and Fundus Photography.
  • A Scan and B Scan for detailed insights into eye structures.
  • Humphrey Field Analyzer (HFA) for glaucoma and optic nerve disorders.
  • Non-Contact Tonometry (NCT) for painless intraocular pressure measurements.
  • Fluorescein Angiography for evaluating retinal blood vessels.
    Vision Correction Services
  • Prescription of high-quality glasses and branded contact lenses.
  • Custom lenses for specific needs like progressive lenses and anti-glare coatings.
  • LASIK and other refractive surgeries for permanent vision correction.
    Surgeries for Eye Conditions
  • Cataract Surgery: Advanced techniques like phacoemulsification with premium intraocular lenses.
  • Glaucoma Surgery: Minimally invasive procedures to control intraocular pressure.
  • Corneal Transplants: Restoring vision for patients with corneal damage.
  • Pterygium Surgery: Removal of abnormal tissue growth on the eye's surface.
  • Chalazion Removal: Quick and painless procedures for eyelid lumps.
  • YAG Laser Capsulotomy: Clearing posterior capsule opacification for cataract patients.
    Treatment for Retinal Disorders
  • Laser treatments for diabetic retinopathy and retinal tears.
  • Intravitreal injections for age-related macular degeneration (AMD).
  • Surgical interventions for retinal detachment.
    Specialized Clinics
  • Dry Eye Clinic: Advanced diagnosis and treatment for chronic dry eye syndrome.
  • Contact Lens Clinic: Fitting and guidance for all types of contact lenses.
  • Pediatric Ophthalmology: Specialized care for children’s eye health.
    Emergency Eye Care
  • Management of eye injuries and trauma.
  • Treatment for sudden vision loss or severe infections.
  • Removal of foreign bodies from the eye.
