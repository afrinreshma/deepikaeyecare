  • Cataract Surgery

Problem Overview

    Condition
  • A cataract is the clouding of the eye's natural lens, which lies behind the iris and pupil. This leads to blurry vision, difficulty seeing at night, and sensitivity to light.
    • Causes
  • Aging, diabetes, prolonged use of steroids, excessive exposure to sunlight, or an eye injury.
    • Impact
  • If untreated, cataracts can lead to significant vision loss or even blindness.
    Surgery Details:

    Procedure

  • Phacoemulsification (Phaco): The most advanced technique for cataract removal.
  • A small incision is made, and the cloudy lens is emulsified using ultrasound waves.
  • An artificial intraocular lens (IOL) is implanted to restore clear vision.
    Duration:
  • The surgery typically takes 15-30 minutes and is performed under local anesthesia.
    Technology Used
  • We use state-of-the-art machines for precision and safety.
    Pre and Post-Surgery Care

    Pre-Surgery:

  • Comprehensive diagnostic tests to plan the procedure.
  • Instructions on medications and fasting, if required.

    Post-Surgery:

  • Protective eyewear to shield the operated eye.
  • Regular follow-ups to monitor healing.
  • Guidance on lifestyle adjustments and medication usage.

  • Expert surgeons with extensive experience in cataract surgeries.
  • Minimal surgical costs without compromising on quality.
  • Use of premium-quality imported lenses for optimal results.
  • Holistic care under one roof.
